# Super Mario 64 star completability

Generated by `scripts/sm64-star-audit.py` from the exported course JSON (`assets/courses`), the sim behavior registry (`native/sim/src/object.rs`, `behaviors/mod.rs`) and the star director (`native/src/progress/stars.rs`). Do not edit by hand: re-run the script after changing an exporter or a behavior (`--check` fails when the file is stale).

* **94 of 120** power stars have their real decomp mechanism in the engine today (115 course / secret stars + 5 castle stars = 120, the game's 120).
* **21** audited stars are blocked by a missing mechanism (below), and the 5 castle stars (Toad, MIPS) are blocked by missing NPC behaviors.
* "stand-in" = the engine still lets that act finish through the course's generic goal star (`star_location` in `stars.rs`), so the star count and saves work, but the decomp objective is not what unlocks it.
* 3 cap switches: wing (Tower of the Wing Cap), metal (Cavern of the Metal Cap), vanish (Vanish Cap under the Moat) all exist as ground switches that set `SAVE_FLAG_HAVE_*_CAP` and unlock the matching ! boxes (see the table at the end).
* "completable" means the star exists in the engine with the decomp mechanism wired and every behavior the audit knows it needs is present; it does not prove that every platform on the way is traversable (see the per-course lists of decomp behaviors without a sim port).
