Status vocabulary: **Ported (bit-exact)** = file-for-file port checked against golden fixtures generated from the decomp-derived TS (the TS itself is checked against the decomp by reading, not by an emulator); **Ported (approximate)** = same intent, different implementation or numbers; **Partial**; **Not ported**; **Out of scope** (N64 hardware, debug, localisation, things the project deliberately replaces). `unknown` means I could not determine it from the repo.

## Biggest gaps / suggested next ports (ranked by gameplay impact)

1. **SM64 swimming set: done** (all 32 submerged actions + `ACT_HOLD_WATER_JUMP`, `native/sim/src/actions/water.rs`). Still open around it: the DDD second whirlpool (needs the Bowser-2 flag in the course request), sounds/particles of the water actions (events only for stroke, kick, plunge), electric shock sources (no Amp or Mr. I exists to call `shock_in_water`), the camera water modes, and the poison-gas branch of `update_mario_health`.
2. **SM64 enemy/obstacle behaviors not ported**: Thwomp, Chuckya, Bullet Bill (WF cannon is a static model), Ukiki, Mips, Scuttlebug, Fly Guy, Moneybag, Tweester, Hoot, Bubba, Eyerok, Mr. I, Boo-in-castle, Unagi, Manta Ray, Chirp Chirp. (Group 2 ported Grindel, Spindel, the circling Amp, the Fire Piranha Plant and the Sushi shark.) 123 of 198 distinct behaviors placed in exported levels have no explicit handling; most of LLL, SSL, WDW, RR, DDD, JRB, BITDW/FS/S and the castle grounds are scenery-only.
3. **SM64 `camera.c` leftovers**: the camera is ported (`native/sim/src/sm64cam/`: 14 of 15 `CAMERA_MODE`s, 31 of 48 `CUTSCENE_*`, the per-level trigger tables). Still missing: the intro / ending / credits / grand star / end-waving / Bowser-arena-entrance / cap-switch / key-door-unlock / pyramid-explosion / dialog / read-message cutscene cameras, the R-button camera, pause zoom-out and the poison-gas camera height.
4. **SM64 cutscene and special actions** (largely done, see the Cutscene row): all 51 cutscene `ACT_*` have sim handlers; still open: sound / particle cues, the camera side (intro spline + Lakitu, credits orbits), time stop for object freezing, the title / file-select levels (the shell replaces them) and wiring the remaining triggers (BBH entrance, Bubba, shock sources, snow stuck) into the enemy / level work.
5. **SM64 levels/areas leftovers**: the 3 cap levels (`cotmc`, `wmotr`, `vcutm`), the castle courtyard and 10 sub-areas are now exported with warps (see `native/SM64_STARS.md` for the completable count). Round 2B added the moat drain (VCutM), the Toad and MIPS castle stars, the PSS timer star, blue-coin and ! box coins for the 100-coin stars and THI's Koopa the Quick race. The last-stars round added Ukiki + cage (TTM act 2), the HMC rolling rocks, the LLL volcano platforms on track, the Big Chill Bully star and the literal fire-piranha star: `native/SM64_STARS.md` now counts 120 / 120 stars by mechanism. Still open: the dynamic-stage mechanisms (WDW water level, TTC clock hands, THI size change, LLL volcano, SSL pyramid, RR carpets), which behavior agents are porting.
6. **MK64 course props**: all 20 courses (16 race tracks, 4 battle arenas) are exported as geometry, textures, collision and lap path (`scripts/mk64_courses.py`), walkable in the LEVELS tab and drivable in the kart mode (16 tracks). Still missing per course: props (trees, signs, Yoshi Valley branching, Wario Stadium/Kalimari trains, Toad's Turnpike traffic, Banshee Boardwalk bats, Rainbow Road, Bowser's Castle thwomps, Koopa Troopa Beach crabs), water/lava as volumes, the in-game minimaps and skies (palettes are hand-picked), (done: per-surface tyre behaviour, boost ramps, fluid levels, sky and fog read from the decomp's `yamls/courses/*_metadata.yml` instead of hand-picked palettes; the in-game minimaps are now the ROM textures, see the Real minimap row).
7. **MK64 driving completeness**: AB-spin, terrain tumble triggering, triple-A/B combos (per-character surface rows are done). Done: boost ramps and the tyre surfaces (`kart_surface.rs`). Done (`kart_effects.rs`): spinouts and the item part of `apply_effect` (banana swerve/spin, green-shell / red-shell / fake-box tumbles, star, boo, lightning shrink, squish, mushroom), kart-to-kart collision (`func_802903D8`), start boost and early-start wobble (window approximate). Items: all 15 (`kart_items.rs`).
8. **MK64 CPU AI and modes**: CPU multi-path sections (Yoshi Valley) and vehicle avoidance, ramp boosts (CPUs need a Lakitu at each jump), staff ghosts, split-screen VS / Battle, replays. Done: Grand Prix cups with points and trophies, Time Trials with own ghosts, hot-seat VS, Balloon Battle on the four arenas, the front-end menus (`kart_modes.rs`, `kart_flow.rs`, `kart_battle.rs`, `kart_ghost.rs`, `kart_menu.rs`).
9. **OoT Link/z_player.c**: 88 of 89 `Player_Action_*` functions have no counterpart (ledge climbing, swimming/diving, crawl, ladders, hookshot, bow, bombs, boomerang, ocarina, spin attack, jump slash; Epona is now a separate port, see the row below). Plus the item/inventory/pause system and every dungeon other than the Deku Tree.
10. **BK**: everything beyond the 9 imported moves: 148 of 157 `BS_*` states (Talon Trot, Wonderwing, eggs, Beak Bomb, flight, Wading Boots, Turbo Trainers, Shock Spring are all in the 20-odd moves; swimming has the BK air meter and climbing is ported since 2026-11-09, see `native/BANJO_CONTROLLER.md`), the collectibles exist and the lair's jigsaw podiums take and return Jiggies (Mumbo's five transformations exist as models + speed caps), 12 enemy species now exist in the 14 worlds (the other character/enemy classes stay unported), 104 BK maps exported (the 14 worlds + 90 sub-areas) with their enemy and collectible placements and 361 warps (doors, pipes, windows, lair doors, return pads) that join them, plus the climb poles, molehills and note doors, but no other door / switch actors, no BK music or sound engine; only Banjo and Kazooie's voice effects (13 `SFX_*` sounds) are extracted, by `scripts/export-character-voices.py`.

## How to update this tally

1. When a port lands, change only the affected row's **Status** and **Note** (rows are stable on purpose, so diffs stay small). Do not reorder rows or rewrite whole paragraphs.
2. Re-run `node scripts/decomp-tally-check.mjs` (read-only, no dependencies). It prints: SM64 action counts by group and the missing list, function-name overlap per decomp file, behavior and level counts, per-level placements, animation/audio/surface/actor counts, the MK64/OoT/BK equivalents (course/scene/state/function counts, items, actor overlays). Paste changed numbers into the matching rows and the Summary table. `--json` prints the same data for diffing (`node scripts/decomp-tally-check.mjs --json > /tmp/new.json`).
3. The MK64, OoT and BK counts need those submodules populated (`git submodule update --init reference/mk64 reference/oot reference/banjo-kazooie`). From a worktree without them, point at a checkout: `MK64_DIR=… OOT_DIR=… BK_DIR=… SM64_DIR=… node scripts/decomp-tally-check.mjs`. When a decomp is absent the script says so and the old numbers stay.
4. Judgement items (marked "judgement" in the Summary and a few "unknown"s) are not computed by the script; revisit them by hand and replace "unknown" with a measurement when someone checks.
5. Update the Summary percentages and the date/commit line at the top, keep the "Biggest gaps" list ranked by gameplay impact, and mention the change in the same commit as the port.
6. Caveats: function-name overlap is an upper bound for "ported" (comments count) and a lower bound for renamed or table-driven ports; "explicitly named by the exporter" is a proxy for behavior coverage, not proof of fidelity.
