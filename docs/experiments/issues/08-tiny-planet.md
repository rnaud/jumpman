## 08 — Tiny Planet

A spherical world 2,200 units in radius that you can run all the way around in about fifteen seconds.

- [x] Mario's physics stay on a flat local floor. Each tick the planet rotates under him by exactly the arc he moved, and he is shifted back to the centre of his frame with his interpolation and the camera, so gravity always points at the core.
- [x] Stone pillars (with coins on top) are re-projected into Mario's frame every tick for collision. Trees, an equator belt of coins, a moon and a star field wheel past as you run.
- [x] HUD: laps around the planet, distance travelled, coins.
